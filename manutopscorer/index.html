ManuTopScorer – Privacy Policy Last updated: 24 November 2026 ManuTopScorer is a motivation app for families. Children log goals (finished tasks) and yellow cards, parents set rewards and home chores, and the app turns each day's score into points. This policy explains what information the app handles. Who is responsible Anas Kamleh, the Netherlands. Contact: anaskamleh@hotmail.com What the app stores Parent account details: name, email address and a password (stored as a hash, not in plain text). Child profile details: the child's first name and a PIN code set by the parent. Family data: family code, daily goal and yellow card counts, points, chores, rewards, messages and history. Where it is stored In this version, all of this information is stored only on your own device. The app has no server and does not send this information to the developer or to any other party. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share information. Notifications The app can send reminders and encouraging messages. These are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in your device settings. Children The app is intended to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and no child information leaves the device. Parents should enter only a first name or nickname for a child. Deleting your data You can delete all app data by using the reset option in the app or by uninstalling the app. Because the data lives only on your device, uninstalling removes it permanently. Future changes If a later version adds online features such as syncing between devices, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match. Your rights Because we hold no copy of your data, you control it directly on your device. For any question, contact the email above.
